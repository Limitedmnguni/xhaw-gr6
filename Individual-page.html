Table Mountain Summit Hike

Price: R850 per person

Duration: Half-day (4 Hours)

Difficulty: Challenging trail

Activity Overview

Experience iconic views of Cape Town and the Atlantic Ocean with a certified local mountain guide. This route takes you through dramatic ravines and rich fynbos vegetation right to the summit plateau.

What is Included

  • Accredited Wilderness First Responder Guide
  • Safety briefings and trail route history
  • Light snacks and refreshments
  • Cable car down-ticket support (weather permitting)

What to Bring

  • Sturdy hiking boots or trail shoes
  • Minimum 2 liters of water
  • Sun protection (hat, sunscreen)
  • Windproof/waterproof jacket
